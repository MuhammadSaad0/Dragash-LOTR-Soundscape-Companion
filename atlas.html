About the geography & map key

Original illustrations informed by Christopher Tolkien’s published maps. Each plate has its own regional scale. Forests, relief and building symbols are illustrative. Dotted red lines show approximate journeys; dashed brown lines show roads. Moria’s crossing is underground. Place search shows geography without changing your listening position.
